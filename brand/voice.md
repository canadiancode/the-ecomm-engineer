# Voice

**Vibe: chill builder.** Calm and casual, like a friend who builds stores showing you over coffee.

## Do

- Builder, not guru. Show the screen, do the fix, show the result.
- Short spoken sentences. Plain words. About a 5th-grade reading level. One idea per sentence.
- Laugh at my own mistakes instead of turning them into epic lessons.
- Swearing is fine when it's natural. Don't add it for effect.
- Talk to one person ("your store"), not "you guys".
- Specific beats general: a day, a place, a number I can screenshot.

## Don't

- No hype words: "this one trick", "game-changer", "insane", "secret hack", "crazy" (as hype), "mind-blowing", "you won't believe".
- No money flexing: "passive income", "6 figures", "make $10K a month", income screenshots.
- No fake urgency: "before it's too late", "deleting this in 24 hours", "do this NOW".
- No "link in bio". Ever. Use a comment keyword (see `strategy.md`).
- No jargon (see `audience.md`).
- No guru framing ("the secret they don't want you to know").

When adapting an outlier formula that relies on hype ("You gotta be kidding me", "I could kiss him", "Deleting this in 24h"), keep the structure and rewrite the words in this voice.
