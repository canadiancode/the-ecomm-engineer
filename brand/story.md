# My story

Info alone is replaceable; my path isn't. Every video carries a piece of this story, and some videos are only the story.

**Short version:** Not sure what I wanted to do → Japan → Whistler → forestry (BCIT, timber cruising, City of Burnaby; everyone said I was crazy to quit) → Yellow Pages → my own apparel store → Shopify merchant support → bootcamp → developer → lead.

## Chapters

Only use facts written here. Anything not here is "(TO CONFIRM)" in a script. Never invent a detail, date, quote or feeling.

### 1. Japan (right after high school)
- No idea what I wanted to do, so I went to Japan for 6 months.
- 2 months as a cook and room cleaner at a ski resort to save up.
- 2 months travelling the country.
- 2 months at a Japanese BBQ in the heart of Tokyo.
- I'm half Japanese (said on camera in the V010 draft). Long-term goal: more time in Japan.

### 2. Whistler (one year)
- Still didn't know what I wanted, so I lived in Whistler for a year.
- Worked 2 jobs, including Brew Creek Lodge, and saved for school.
- A summer spent in nature made me decide to give forestry a go.
- Mountain biking and snowboarding (photos exist).

### 3. Forestry
- Studied Forest and Natural Areas Management at BCIT.
- Worked as a timber cruiser in Prince George and Mackenzie, BC.
- About a year with City of Burnaby forestry, rebuilding trail networks.
- When I quit to chase tech, everyone said I was crazy to throw a steady job away.
- **The moment:** in a work camp in Mackenzie, BC, teaching myself tech on terrible wifi, with no freedom over where I lived.

## Why I'm sharing this

Social media makes everyone feel behind. I've done a lot, and I still feel behind. So this isn't a "made it" account. It's the journey in real time, for the people still grinding who don't have it all figured out.

## The belief (the thread through every story)

- Your 20s are for finding what you love to do.
- Your 30s are for getting really good at it.
- Your 40s are for capitalizing on it.

This account is that process, happening in real time.

## Story rules

1. One chapter per video, never the whole story at once. (Exception: Story ep 1, the 60-second About Me.)
2. Specific moments over big lessons: a day, a place, a line someone said.
3. Laugh at the struggle. No rags-to-riches, no income flexing.
4. My current employer stays unnamed: "the stores I work on".
5. Every story ends with what it means for the viewer and their store.
6. Never invent facts about my life. Unknowns are "(TO CONFIRM)".
7. Value videos get one personal line from a chapter ("When I was at Shopify, I saw…").
