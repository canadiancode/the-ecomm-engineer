One video a day. Instagram first, reposted to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn. Built on Ava Yuergens' outlier method from Personal Brand Launch (see the `pbl-method` skill).

## Weekly mix (of ~7 posts)

- **40% story (~3):** the Forestry to Shopify series, building The Ecomm Engineer in public, wins and mistakes. Stories drive shares and follows.
- **40% value through my experience (~3):** proven outlier formats, each with one personal line ("When I was at Shopify, I saw…"). Lessons drive comments and leads.
- **20% news (~1–2):** from the daily scan, filmed same day, with my take.

## CTA ladder

| Level | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Bad | "Follow me!" |
| Good | "Follow for more Shopify fixes." |
| Great | "Want the 10 fixes I'd make on any Shopify store this week? Comment CRO and I'll send you my checklist." + proof on screen (keyword TO CONFIRM, see Lead magnets) |

- **Value videos:** comment a keyword → ManyChat sends the DM. Keyword is **7 letters or fewer**, one per lead magnet, in caps.
- **Story videos:** a follow CTA tied to the series ("Next: … Follow along."). Pipeline column L = `Follow`.
- Never "link in bio".

## Captions

The caption does a different job from the video. It adds context, fine print, a series counter ("Story 3/10.") or a question. It never repeats the spoken hook.

## Stories and profile

- 3 to 5 IG Stories a week: behind the scenes, tool demos, polls, lead-magnet keyword.
- Bio: who I help, the result, Shopify credibility.
- Pin 3 posts: Story ep 1, best value video, freebie.

## Winners

- A **winner** is a video with **≥ 5× my platform average views** (threshold in `'Start here'!B5`; flagged by the Analytics "Winner" formula).
- Winners get re-filmed with a new hook and in new formats (whiteboard, two-person, voiceover over B-roll) and noun-swapped for other store types.
- Re-films are new Pipeline rows: Idea ID = the original Video ID (e.g. `V007`), Notes start with `Re-film of V007:`.

## Weekly loop

| When | What happens | Kevin's time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every morning | `/research` (scheduled) → Ideas | 0 |
| Same day, as needed | News bullets, 10-minute phone shoot | 10–15 min |
| Fri/Sun | `/weekly`: stats → Analytics, winners, ~10 scripts for Saturday | 20 min to approve |
| Saturday | Batch shoot. Say the Video ID at the start of every take. Re-film winners. | 4–6 hrs |
| After the shoot | Raw clips to Drive → `/edit <ID>` → Ready for review | 0 |
| Daily | Kevin approves on phone → `/publish <ID>` | 1 min per video |
