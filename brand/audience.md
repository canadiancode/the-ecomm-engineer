# Audience

The viewer is a person, not a segment.

- **Primary:** a Shopify store owner doing roughly $5K to $100K a month, running it alone or with 1 to 5 people, wearing every hat.
- **Secondary:** someone launching their first store who wants to skip the beginner mistakes.
- **Not for:** enterprise brands with in-house teams, or get-rich-quick dropshippers.

**The promise:** make more, spend less, ship faster.

Rule: broad hook, narrow value, niche CTA, so the people who act are store owners.

- "Shipping is eating my margins."
- "I don't show up on Google, or in ChatGPT."
- "I'm doing everything myself and I'm drowning."

The objections they always raise (answer one in every value video):

- "I'm not technical." → show it's clicks, not code.
- "I don't have time." → show how long the fix takes.
- "That only works for big stores." → do it on the small demo store.

## Jargon to avoid

| Never say | Say instead |
| --- | --- |
| CRO, conversion rate optimization | Getting more visitors to buy |
| AOV | How much people spend per order |
| AEO | Showing up when people ask ChatGPT |
| CAC / LTV | What a customer costs you / what they're worth over time |
| 3PL | A shipping warehouse that packs your orders |
| Liquid, metafields, theme code | Your store's design / extra product info |
| Headless, Hydrogen, API | Leave out, or show it working instead |

This applies to spoken lines, on-screen text and captions. (Open question: the lead-magnet keyword "CRO" breaks this list. See `strategy.md`.)
