- **My current employer, the agency I work with, its stores, clients, numbers, suppliers and internal methods.** Not in content, files, captions, Sheet cells, Drive filenames, Descript projects or commit messages. Say "the stores I work on". Work screens never appear on camera.

- Real revenue, sales or profit numbers, or "I made $X". Only demo-store numbers go on screen.
